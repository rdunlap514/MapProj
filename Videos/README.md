# MapProj video tutorials

**[Watch the combined walkthrough on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-dAHxD8eRYA)** for convenient playback.

## Original recordings

Five short recordings walk through setting up MapProj and managing your building. Watch in order, or jump to the task you need. Total runtime: approximately **4 minutes 49 seconds**.

1. **[Logo setup](mapproj%20-%201%20-%20Logo.mp4)** — 0:34. Set up the app's logo.
2. **[Create a building](mapproj%20-%202%20-%20building%20creation.mp4)** — 0:22. Name a building and upload its floor plan.
3. **[Room calibration](mapproj%20-%203%20-%20Room%20Calibration.mp4)** — 0:54. Trace and save rooms on the floor plan.
4. **[Edit the legend](mapproj%20-%204%20-%20legend%20editor.mp4)** — 0:49. Customize status names, colors, and the modifier.
5. **[Manage users](mapproj%20-%205%20-%20users.mp4)** — 2:10. Work with accounts and the administrator's user controls.

Open a video link to view the file; download it if your browser does not offer playback. The recordings are 1080p MP4 files.

The videos demonstrate customized labels. A fresh building starts with RoomState1, RoomState2, Roomstate3, and Modifier; rename them to suit your workflow.

For written steps, see [the room creation walkthrough](../docs/ROOM_WALKTHROUGH.md). For installation and account setup, see [the main README](../README.md).
