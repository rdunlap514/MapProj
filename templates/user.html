Welcome to MapProj

Mark room maintenance progress across buildings in real-time.

Quick Start:

  1. Select Building — Choose from the dropdown
  2. Click Any Room — Opens color/status options
  3. Mark Status — Choose the current room color
  4. Add Notes — Leave notes for admin to review

Status Colors (Customizable by Admin):

  • Check the legend in the bottom-right for current building's status meanings
  • Colors may vary per building

💡 Navigation Tips:

  • Scroll wheel to zoom in/out
  • Middle-click drag to pan the map
